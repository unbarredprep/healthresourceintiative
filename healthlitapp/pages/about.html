About ClearCare

Healthcare in plain language

Built for the Congressional App Challenge 2026. Built for the people who need it most.

Every day, people leave a doctor's office more confused than when they walked in.

They receive a diagnosis they don't understand, a prescription they can't read, and discharge papers written in jargon. They go home with unanswered questions — and that confusion has real consequences.

ClearCare exists to fix that. We translate medical documents into plain language, help people prepare for appointments, and connect them to free care in their community — in any language, for anyone.

Important note
ClearCare is not a medical provider. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace your doctor. We help you understand information your doctor already gave you. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for medical advice.
