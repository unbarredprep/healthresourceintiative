Who this is for

Built for the people
left behind

Every major health app was built for people who already understand the system. ClearCare starts from zero — and builds everything from there.

Low-income families
Find sliding-scale care, understand assistance programs, and avoid costly ER visits with the right information.
Non-English speakers
The full experience in your language from the start — not a translation button added as an afterthought.
Elderly individuals
Large text, simple navigation, and a warm interface that treats you with dignity rather than assuming tech comfort.
Uninsured people
No navigator, no idea what anything costs? ClearCare shows you what you can actually access, right now.
Rural communities
Telehealth options and verified resources for people where distance is a real barrier, not an inconvenience.
